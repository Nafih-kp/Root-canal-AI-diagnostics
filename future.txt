Knowledge distillation typically involves:

Teacher model: Larger YOLO model (e.g., YOLOv8m, YOLOv8l)
Student model: Smaller YOLO model (e.g., YOLOv8n)
Distillation process: Transfer knowledge from teacher to student
Benefits: Smaller, faster model with better accuracy than raw training
